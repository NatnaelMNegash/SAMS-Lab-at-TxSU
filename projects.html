Research projects

Current project directions organized around the lab’s three research thrusts.

01 / AUTONOMY

Human-aware driving

Investigating interaction-aware decision-making for autonomous vehicles sharing roads with pedestrians, cyclists, and human drivers.

02 / CONTROL

Safety-critical motion control

Exploring intelligent control strategies that incorporate system dynamics, uncertainty, and safety constraints.

03 / MOBILITY

Connected mobility systems

Studying network coordination, routing, and energy-aware scheduling for fleets of ground and aerial robots.

These are research directions, not claims of completed or funded projects.
